# Q5. Bit Operations

## Problem Description

Given:

- **a = 1010011**
- **b = 1001001**

Find their **OR**, **XOR**, and **AND**.

### Options

- A. OR = 1011011, XOR = 0011010, AND = 1000001
- B. OR = 1011101, XOR = 1011010, AND = 1000001
- C. OR = 1011011, XOR = 1000001, AND = 0011010
- D. OR = 1011101, XOR = 1000001, AND = 0011010
